Capital partners

We build in cash rather than on construction debt. That means faster closes on land, better pricing from subcontractors, and no interest accruing while the framing goes up. The tradeoff is that we work with partners instead of a bank.

  • You bringCommitted capital per project or across a portfolio
  • You getA preferred return plus equity in the upside
  • SecurityYour position is tied to a specific, deeded property
  • ReportingDraw schedules, site progress and budget-to-actual every month

How a project runs

Land in, keys out.

Five phases. Partners see the same schedule we do, start to finish.

Phase 01 · Terms

We agree on the split before anything moves

Lot review, a build concept sized to the site, and a written partnership agreement covering contribution, distribution and timeline.

Phase 02 · Design

Plans drawn for what actually sells here

Architecture, engineering and selections tuned to what Teton Valley buyers pay for — not a plan pulled off a shelf.

Phase 03 · Permit

County, HOA and utilities cleared

We carry the entitlement work, the HOA design review and the utility coordination. Partners don't sit in those meetings.

Phase 04 · Build

Construction managed in house

Our own construction arm runs the job, so schedule and budget stay under one roof. Monthly progress and budget-to-actual go out to every partner.

Phase 05 · Sale

Listed, sold, distributed

Staging, marketing and the listing are ours. Proceeds are distributed per the agreement at closing.

Why the west slope

We only build where we live.

Teton Valley sits over the pass from Jackson with the same mountains and a fraction of the price. Buyers who priced out of Wyoming keep landing here, and the supply of well-built, finished homes hasn't caught up.

  • Victor, Driggs and Teton Springs — the only markets we work in
  • Relationships with subcontractors, the county and local brokers, built over 10+ years
  • Construction held in house rather than bid out to a third-party GC
  • Every partner gets the same monthly reporting we run the business on
