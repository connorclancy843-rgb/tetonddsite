Where we build · Alta, Wyoming

Alta, Wyoming — Teton Valley's west-slope corner.

Alta is the one Wyoming town in Teton Valley, tucked against the west slope of the Tetons and reached through Idaho. It sits closest to Grand Targhee Resort, leans to larger parcels and mountain settings, and rounds out the valley where we develop land and build homes.

About the town

Wyoming address, Idaho valley.

Alta is a geographic curiosity: it's in Teton County, Wyoming, but it sits on the Idaho side of the range and is reached by road only through Driggs. Day to day, Alta functions as part of Teton Valley — its children attend Idaho-side schools and most services come from Driggs a few minutes away — while the property itself carries a Wyoming address and Wyoming's tax treatment.

It's also the closest community to Grand Targhee Resort, the ski area on the back of the Tetons, which gives Alta a specific appeal for buyers who want mountain access and larger, more rural parcels than the townsites offer. For a developer, Alta is a smaller, more deliberate market — the right lot matters more than volume.

The tradeoffs that come with Alta are the flip side of its appeal. Parcels here are more likely to need a well and septic than a municipal hookup, access roads see real winter, and the Wyoming county process is its own paperwork even though your neighbors, schools and grocery run are all Idaho. None of that is a dealbreaker — it's simply why we look hard at each site's fundamentals before we commit to building. When the access, water and building envelope line up, an Alta lot can deliver a setting the townsites can't.
