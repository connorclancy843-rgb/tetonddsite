New construction in Victor, Idaho — the first town over the pass.

Victor sits at the base of Teton Pass, about a half-hour drive from Jackson Hole and the closest Teton Valley town to it. We develop land and build spec homes here — several of our projects, including 88 and 90 Hastings Drive and 681 Lucia Lane, are in Victor.

About the town

Why Victor is where a lot of Teton Valley building happens.

Of the Teton Valley towns, Victor is the one closest to Jackson Hole — Teton Pass (Highway 33 to Wyoming's 22) drops you into Jackson in good conditions in well under an hour. That proximity makes Victor a natural landing spot for people who work on the Wyoming side but want to own on the Idaho side, where land and finished homes cost a fraction of comparable property across the range.

The town itself has grown from a quiet ranching stop into a walkable small-town core with a brewery, restaurants and services, while newer subdivisions ring the edges with buildable residential lots. That mix — an established town center plus room to build — is exactly the kind of setting our development model is built for.

Working with us

Own a Victor lot, or want to buy a finished home?

If you own a buildable lot in Victor and would rather turn it into a finished home than sell the dirt, our landowner partnership lets you contribute the lot to a build and take a share of the profit at closing — no capital out of pocket and no contractor to manage. If you'd rather put capital behind a build, our capital-partner page explains how that works.

Looking to buy? Our new-construction homes page tracks what's finished and for sale, and the buying-land guide walks through choosing a building lot in the valley. And if what you really want is a builder to construct a custom home on land you already own and plan to keep, that's a different job than ours — our sister company Artistic Builders handles custom builds for hire.
