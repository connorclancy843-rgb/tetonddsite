Where we build · Driggs, Idaho

New construction and development in Driggs, Idaho — the valley's county seat.

Driggs is the largest town in Teton Valley and the seat of Teton County, Idaho — where the county offices, the regional airport and much of the valley's commerce sit. We develop land and build spec homes here, including 475 Silver Fox Lane, currently under construction.

About the town

The valley's hub — with a straight-on view of the Grand.

Driggs is the practical center of Teton Valley. As the county seat it's where permitting and county services happen, and Driggs-Reed Memorial Airport gives the town small-aircraft access that the other valley towns don't have. Main Street is the valley's most developed commercial stretch, with restaurants, shops and services that stay open year-round.

For a developer, Driggs offers something specific: from the west side of the valley you look straight across at the back of the Teton range — the same peaks Jackson Hole faces, seen from the quieter side. That view, combined with in-town infrastructure and buildable lots in and around the townsite, is why Driggs is a steady part of our pipeline.

Driggs also gives buyers the shortest path to everyday life in the valley. In-town lots can tie into municipal services, the walkable core means you're not driving for coffee, groceries or dinner, and the airport and county offices are minutes away. That mix of convenience and view is why finished, move-in-ready homes here are consistently in demand — and why a well-sited new build in Driggs tends to find its buyer.
